Manufacturing · Remote (TX)

Packaging Sales Representative

Base salary + uncapped commission — DOE · Reports to Owner

Location Remote — Houston, Dallas, Austin, or San Antonio, TX
Type Permanent
Salary Base + uncapped commission
Sector Manufacturing & Sales

Our client is a corrugated packaging manufacturer offering a wide range of box sizes and styles to meet diverse business needs. Their solutions are designed to support the safe transport, protection, and presentation of products across multiple industries. Backed by years of experience, they are known for delivering high-quality products and exceptional customer service.

In addition to their manufacturing capabilities, they also offer a full suite of packaging solutions through their distribution network, providing a comprehensive, one-stop approach for their customers. The business is in an exciting growth phase, having recently acquired a new facility and invested in new equipment — significantly increasing capacity as they continue to scale.

We are seeking an ambitious, results-driven packaging sales professional to join the team and drive growth across a defined territory in Texas.

Key Responsibilities

  • Drive new business and expand existing accounts across manufacturing, distribution, and industrial sectors within your Texas territory
  • Manage the full sales cycle: prospecting, needs analysis, quoting, closing, and ongoing account management
  • Sell value-added, customised packaging solutions rather than commodity products — aligning packaging designs with customer operational and cost objectives
  • Collaborate with internal production and design teams to deliver efficient, tailored solutions
  • Manage RFQs, pricing strategies, and sales forecasts through CRM tools
  • Maintain strong knowledge of key target industries: industrial manufacturing, logistics, energy, and food & beverage
  • Build and nurture long-term client relationships through strong communication, negotiation, and follow-through
  • Operate independently within a defined territory while reporting directly to the Owner

Requirements

  • Prior packaging sales experience is required
  • Corrugated experience preferred, but manufacturing or distribution backgrounds will also be considered
  • Self-starter with an entrepreneurial mindset, strong ambition, and the drive to build a territory
  • Established professional network within Houston, Dallas, Austin, or San Antonio
  • Proven track record of driving new business and growing existing accounts
  • Strong communication, negotiation, and relationship-building skills
  • Comfortable working independently and managing your own pipeline and priorities
  • Must be based near one of the four listed Texas cities

Why Apply

  • Join a business in active growth — new facility, new equipment, and strong ownership backing
  • Uncapped commission structure with genuine earning potential
  • Entrepreneurial culture with direct access to the owner — no corporate bureaucracy
  • A product people actually need: corrugated packaging with proven demand across multiple industries
