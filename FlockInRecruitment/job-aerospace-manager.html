Engineering · Orange County, CA

Business Unit Manager — Aerospace Manufacturing

$175,000 – $200,000 / year · On-site · Full-time

Location Orange County, CA (on-site)
Type Permanent
Salary $175,000 – $200,000 / yr
Sector Aerospace & Manufacturing

This is a senior leadership role within an established aerospace manufacturing business. The Business Unit Manager will have full accountability for the performance of the business unit — covering operations, quality, safety, people, and P&L. The role sits at the intersection of strategic planning and hands-on operational leadership, and reports directly to senior executive leadership.

Day-to-day activities span production scheduling, resource allocation, financial oversight, new product introductions, and maintaining strong customer and supplier relationships. The right candidate will be a confident, experienced leader who is equally comfortable in a boardroom conversation and on the shop floor.

Key Responsibilities

  • Oversee all aspects of the aerospace manufacturing business unit, ensuring operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, and profitable growth
  • Manage production schedules, quality standards, and safety practices across the unit
  • Coordinate closely with engineering, supply chain, and finance teams to align priorities and resources
  • Lead and develop a cross-functional team — setting performance goals, coaching individuals, and managing outcomes
  • Monitor KPIs and operational metrics to identify improvement opportunities and drive continuous improvement
  • Review financial reports, manage budgets, and take ownership of the P&L for the business unit
  • Support new product introductions from concept through to production readiness
  • Maintain strong, trust-based relationships with aerospace customers and key suppliers
  • Ensure compliance with all regulatory and industry requirements, including aerospace certifications
  • Prepare reports and strategic updates for senior leadership; contribute to long-term business planning

Requirements

  • Proven experience in aerospace or advanced manufacturing operations, including leadership of a business unit, department, or plant
  • Demonstrated ability to manage production, quality, and safety processes at scale
  • Familiarity with lean manufacturing or continuous improvement methodologies (e.g., Six Sigma, Kaizen)
  • Strong financial acumen — budgeting, cost control, and P&L interpretation to guide business decisions
  • Demonstrated people leadership: team building, performance management, coaching, and cross-functional collaboration
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills — comfortable presenting to customers, suppliers, and executive leadership
  • Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Business, Operations Management, or a related field; advanced degree is a plus
  • Knowledge of aerospace industry standards and certifications such as AS9100 and ITAR is highly beneficial
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to drive data-informed decisions
  • Comfortable with on-site, hands-on leadership in a manufacturing environment in Orange County, CA

Why Apply

  • Senior leadership role with full business unit accountability — a genuine opportunity to own outcomes
  • Competitive compensation of $175,000–$200,000 reflecting the seniority and scope of the role
  • Work within a respected aerospace manufacturing environment with strong industry standing
  • Direct line to senior leadership with real influence over strategy and direction
